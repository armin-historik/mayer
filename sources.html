The Josef Mayer Research Project

Sources and research notes

Primary evidence, retrospective accounts, image provenance and open questions.

Primary technical evidence

  1. United States Patent Office, Josef Mayer, Permanent-hair-waving appliance, US 1,619,794 (filed 19 March 1925; issued 1 March 1927; earliest stated priority 3 April 1924).
  2. United States patent reissue US RE18,841, pending detailed comparison of the original and reissued claims.
  3. Historical advertisement for Mayer Realistic Karlsbad, from the REALISTIC image collection; publication and date not yet identified.
  4. Die Mayerwelle und ihre Vorzüge, historical trade-press page 20, research record DW-MAYER-001. Periodical, author and publication date not yet identified.

Open research questions

  1. Locate original civil records for Mayer’s birth and death and independent documentation of his early travels.
  2. Identify the date and original publication of the Karlsbad advertisement and the factory photograph.
  3. Locate the complete original article Die Mayerwelle und ihre Vorzüge and its continuation.
  4. Identify the people and occasion in the salon group photograph.
  5. Establish which surviving apparatuses correspond to specific patented or commercially sold models.
  6. Locate contemporary evidence of contact, collaboration or disputes between Nessler and Mayer.
  7. Compare the full national patent family, including reissues and claim amendments.
Method: A contemporary advertisement establishes what was advertised, not necessarily the truth of every promotional claim. A patent documents a disclosed invention, not sales or universal priority. A retrospective biography is valuable evidence but should be corroborated independently.

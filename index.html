Josef Mayer: Life and Work

Josef Mayer was a hairdresser, inventor and entrepreneur associated with the development of permanent wave techniques in the early twentieth century. His work is especially connected with flat-winding methods and the Mayer Realistic system.

This project examines his professional career, inventions and international connections through historical documents and archival research.

The Mayer Realistic System

Flat-Winding

Mayer's flat-winding approach is associated with developments in permanent waving during 1924.

Karlsbad, 1927

The Mayer Realistic enterprise was established in Karlsbad in 1927.

International Context

The system forms part of the wider history of technical exchange and commercial innovation in professional hairdressing.

The Josef Mayer Research Project

Josef Mayer (1881–1952)

The hairdresser, inventor and entrepreneur behind the Mayer Realistic permanent-wave system.

Josef Mayer developed and promoted an influential approach to permanent waving in the 1920s. Working in Karlsbad (today Karlovy Vary), he combined technical experimentation, a distinctive winding method, apparatus design and professional training.

This independent research site brings together surviving patents, historical advertisements, photographs, equipment and retrospective accounts. It separates documented evidence from claims still awaiting verification.

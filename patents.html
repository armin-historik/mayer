Patents and technical evidence

Primary patent records anchor the history of Mayer’s flat-winding apparatus.

Patent specifications are among the most reliable sources for identifying the construction an inventor disclosed at a particular date. They must not be confused with evidence of first practical use, manufacturing scale or commercial success.

United States patent 1,619,794

InventorJosef Mayer, Karlsbad
TitlePermanent-hair-waving appliance
Earliest stated priority3 April 1924
US filing19 March 1925
Issue date1 March 1927
Technical subjectHinged electrical heater for flat-wound strands of hair

The patent’s illustrations show the hinged heater construction. The patent drawings are linked to the original record rather than reproduced from an unverified local copy.

Reissue patent US RE18,841

A further patent record relevant to the Mayer research is US RE18,841. Its relationship to the original claims and its precise technical scope should be checked against the reissue specification before making comparative claims.

How to read the evidence

Priority dates identify the claimed filing history of a patent family. The legal scope lies in the claims, read together with the description and drawings. National filings and reissues may differ. A thorough technical history must compare these documents rather than treating every patent number as a separate invention.

These materials were supplied for the research project by REALISTIC, a.s., Karlovy Vary. Their publication has been authorised. Descriptions distinguish what is visible from details still requiring historical verification.

Textual source record: DW-MAYER-001

Die Mayerwelle und ihre Vorzüge, surviving page 20, describes the Realistic system, hair division, protective clamps and a sequence of working steps. The article includes two figures and ends with “Fortsetzung folgt.” The journal title, date and missing continuation are not yet identified. The research record is not a substitute for the original page scan.

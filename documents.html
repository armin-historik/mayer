The Josef Mayer Research Project

Historical documents & visual archive

Photographs, certificates, advertisements, apparatuses and original patent records related to Josef Mayer and Mayer Realistic.

This research inventory brings together historical material relating to Josef Mayer (1881–1952), his Realistic permanent-wave system and its professional reception. Images are reproduced with permission from REALISTIC, a.s., Karlovy Vary, where indicated. Undated objects are not assigned speculative years.

How to use this archive. Select an image to open the original file. Captions identify what the item shows, while publication dates, object models and provenance remain explicitly open where they have not been established. The two US patents are available as complete PDFs and as page-by-page scans.

People, premises and the Realistic business

These photographs and advertising materials are relevant to Mayer’s professional career and the organisation of his Karlsbad enterprise. The autobiographical account reproduced at mayer-realistic.de provides additional biographical context, but its first-person claims should be distinguished from independent documentary confirmation.

Training diplomas and professional recognition

The surviving Realistic certificates document the branding and professional teaching of Mayer’s system. They are not Josef Mayer’s personal “Diplôme de Professeur” from Nice dated 2 April 1906. The latter is mentioned in his autobiographical account, but its original certificate is not reproduced here.

Original US patent documents

Unlike the undated collection images, these patent publications carry verifiable filing and publication information. Each page is viewable directly on this website. US Re. 18,841 is not a reissue of US 1,619,794. They belong to different patent lineages.

US 1,619,794 · Permanent-Hair-Waving Appliance

Filed 19 March 1925; Czechoslovak priority 3 April 1924; patented 1 March 1927. Hinged electrically heated appliance. Open the complete original PDF · Detailed patent analysis.

US Re. 18,841 · Hair Waving Method

Reissued 30 May 1933; method patent concerning flat winding, clamps and protective materials. Supplied copy includes a later disclaimer filed in 1938. Open the complete original PDF · Detailed patent analysis.

Film supplied with the REALISTIC collection. Recording date, apparatus identification and original film context remain to be established.

Textual source record · DW-MAYER-001

Die Mayerwelle und ihre Vorzüge, surviving page 20, describes hair division, protective clamps and stages of the Realistic process. The page ends with “Fortsetzung folgt.” The journal title, date and continuation have not yet been securely identified. The source record should not be mistaken for a complete original publication.

Further sources and pending additions

Josef Mayer’s first-person “Lebenslauf + Informationsbericht” is a central source for his training in Nice, the Karlsbad business, events of 1938–1948 and the founding of Mayco in Darmstadt. It is autobiographical testimony and requires critical comparison with other sources. The photograph of Charles Nessler and Josef Mayer together in Berlin in 1939, discussed by Hans Lehmberg (Karl Ludwig Nessler und die Erfindung der Dauerwelle, 2nd ed., 1986, p. 86), will be added when a suitable reproduction and its context have been reviewed.
